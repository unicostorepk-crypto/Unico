Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order? +
Browse our collection of luxury watches or perfumes, click on any product to view details, then select 'Add to Cart' or 'Buy Now'. Provide your delivery address in Pakistan, choose your payment method, and confirm your order.
Which payment methods are accepted? +
We accept JazzCash (03054272499 - Abdullah), Easypaisa (03054272499 - Asad Nadeem), UBL Bank Transfer (A/C: 0463383104492 - Asad Nadeem), and Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan.
How does Cash on Delivery (COD) work? +
When you select Cash on Delivery at checkout, a standard shipping fee of Rs. 150 is added. You pay the exact amount in Pakistani Rupees to the delivery rider at your doorstep.
What are the delivery charges? +
Nationwide delivery is 100% complimentary on all advance payments (JazzCash, Easypaisa, UBL) and orders over Rs. 10,000. Cash on Delivery orders incur a nominal Rs. 150 courier charge.
Can I inquire on WhatsApp before purchasing? +
Yes! Every product has an 'Inquire on WhatsApp' button that directly opens a WhatsApp chat with our boutique concierge at 03054272499 with your chosen item details prefilled.
How do I check my order status? +
Click on 'Me' in the header anytime. Your complete order history with real-time updates (Pending, Approved, Processing, Shipped, Delivered) is saved locally on your browser.
